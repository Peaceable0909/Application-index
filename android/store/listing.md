```
WhiteRock is the student portal of WhiteRock Educational Services. If you have applied to study abroad with us, sign in with the email address you applied with and keep everything about your application in one place.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Follow your application. See which stage each university application has reached, what it means in plain words, and what comes next.
• Send your documents. See exactly which documents your university asks for, upload files or photograph pages with your phone, and see when each one has been received and checked.
• Message your counsellor. Chat with text, photos, files and voice notes, and call your counsellor by voice or video from the app.
• Book your interview slots with the team in the same place.
• Practise for your credibility interview with sample questions and feedback.
• Get a notification when your counsellor replies or calls, or when something needs your attention.
• Choose a light, dark or soft look and the accent colour you like.

SIMPLE, SECURE SIGN-IN
There is no password to remember. We email you a one-time code, or you can continue with Google.

YOUR PRIVACY
Only the WhiteRock team working on your application can see your information. We never sell it or use it for advertising. You can read the full policy at https://applications-2026-kohl.vercel.app/privacy

WHO IS IT FOR
Students who have applied through WhiteRock Educational Services, and WhiteRock team members. If you have not applied yet, send us your documents through the application form or ask a counsellor to help you start.
```
